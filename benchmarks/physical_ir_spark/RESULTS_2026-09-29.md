# GB10 physical-IR regression, 2026-09-29

The final paired campaign passed on Spark `192.168.2.178`, GB10 sm121, driver
580.178.04, CUDA 13.0.88. Baseline is commit
`aad4e7e578b4e2d7f984ce3c9f448f4e90543121`; current is the final working-tree
export for this refactor, including earlier uncommitted work. Raw local output:
`/tmp/lunaflux-physical-ir-spark.21eDJ0/projection-campaign/`.

This is a production-generated **kernel diagnostic**, not a serving benchmark,
vLLM/SGLang comparison, or target-specific runtime admission. Export fixtures
retain sm120/CUDA 13.1 metadata; their source is explicitly recompiled for the
available sm121/CUDA 13.0 hardware.

## Coverage

- QKV, output, selected-row vocabulary head and gated MLP over
  1/8/32/128/129/1024/2048 input tokens: 28 paired shape cases, five
  counterbalanced trials each, bytewise equality and sampled scalar checks.
- MLP reports gate/up, down and the full chain separately.
- QKV and MLP's intermediate/down fold also pass three- and four-stage shared
  rings, 32-wide transfers and two fragment slots at 129/1024 tokens: eight
  additional paired cases. Sibling folding retains its default schedule in
  these variants. Larger requested variants were rejected by the existing
  48 KiB per-block contract before CUDA allocation; refusal logs are retained.
- Before/current full-ingress source at head dimensions 16/32/64/128 each
  passes 1/2/7/8/15/16/17/31/32-token numerical and exact KV-write checks.
- QKV/output/MLP pass memcheck; current head32 ingress passes memcheck,
  racecheck and synccheck. This does not claim sanitizer coverage of all shapes.

## Memory and artifact handling

Every command ran under user-systemd `MemoryMax=8G`, `MemorySwapMax=0`,
`TasksMax=64`, and a 180-second timeout, with `/usr/bin/time -v`. At least 32 GiB
available memory and an empty GPU compute-process list were checked before
each GPU test. No preexisting workload was stopped.

The representative head run's maximum RSS was 1,624,224 KiB (~1.55 GiB); MLP
was 157,224 KiB. Explicit device arrays in the largest head fixture total
about 0.94 GB. Process RSS and cgroup peaks are **not** complete unified GPU
allocation measurements. Available host memory changed from 123,448,428 to
123,449,900 KiB; swap-free stayed exactly 16,443,268 KiB. No OOM occurred and
the terminal compute-process list was empty.

The separate ingress timing campaign peaked at 103,664 KiB process RSS with
less than 3 MiB explicitly allocated device arrays. Available memory changed
from 123,421,700 to 122,917,672 KiB (still above 117 GiB), swap-free stayed
16,443,268 KiB, and its final idle guard passed. The decrease in host available
memory is a host-wide observation, not a measured kernel allocation or leak.

`ARTIFACTS.sha256` records remote generated source and binary identities;
`remote-artifacts/` is the non-overwriting download. `verify_download.mbtx`
rehashes those files locally into `LOCAL_ARTIFACTS.sha256`. All raw standard
output, errors, command lines and exit statuses are retained separately.
